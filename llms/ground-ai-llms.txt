# Ground

> Ground is an AI revenue engine for commerce: autonomous AI agents that live inside a brand's existing marketing and support stack (Klaviyo, Shopify, Attentive, Postscript, Gorgias, Yotpo, Alia) and continuously uncover and unlock revenue across new-customer acquisition and repeat purchases.

Ground connects to the tools ecommerce brands already use and analyzes customer behavior, purchasing patterns, geography, and hundreds of other signals to find and act on revenue opportunities that internal teams cannot compute or execute at scale. It is designed to complement marketing teams, not replace them — a focused layer that captures and boosts revenue on top of existing email, SMS, and lifecycle programs. Ground is used by direct-to-consumer and ecommerce brands and by the agencies that serve them.
